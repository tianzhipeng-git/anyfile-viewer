## SKP · 中文

收到一个 SKP 文件，只想看看模型，却没有安装 SketchUp？Anyfile 可以在浏览器中打开受支持的 SKP 模型，旋转、缩放并检查基础材质。无需注册，本地文件不会上传。

支持最大 128 MiB 的输入文件，需要 WebGL 2。部分 SketchUp 版本和特性不受支持，复杂模型还受几何、纹理与设备内存限制。

[打开 SKP 查看器](https://www.anyfile.top/zh-CN/formats/skp) · [先试用桌椅样例](https://www.anyfile.top/zh-CN/view?entry=%2Fzh-CN%2Fformats%2Fskp#file=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.anyfile.top%2Fsamples%2Fmodel%2Fchair-and-table.skp)

## SKP · English

Received an SKP file but don't have SketchUp installed? Open supported SKP models in Anyfile to rotate, zoom and inspect basic materials in your browser. No account is needed, and your local file is never uploaded.

The input limit is 128 MiB and WebGL 2 is required. Some SketchUp versions and features are unsupported; complex models also depend on geometry, texture and device-memory limits.

[Open the SKP viewer](https://www.anyfile.top/en/formats/skp) · [Try a chair-and-table sample](https://www.anyfile.top/en/view?entry=%2Fen%2Fformats%2Fskp#file=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.anyfile.top%2Fsamples%2Fmodel%2Fchair-and-table.skp)

## PSB · 中文

收到 Photoshop 的 PSB 文件，只需要查看图稿？Anyfile 可以直接在浏览器中预览文件里已保存的合成图，支持缩放和拖动。无需安装 Photoshop 或注册，本地文件不会上传。

仅查看已保存的合成图，不能切换或编辑图层；缺少合成图的文件无法预览。文件上限 256 MiB，合成图上限约 6700 万像素，部分颜色模式或特性可能不支持。

[打开 PSB 查看器](https://www.anyfile.top/zh-CN/formats/psb) · [先试用色彩合成图样例](https://www.anyfile.top/zh-CN/view?entry=%2Fzh-CN%2Fformats%2Fpsb#file=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.anyfile.top%2Fsamples%2Fdesign%2Fcolor-study.psb)

## PSB · English

Received a Photoshop PSB file and just need to see the artwork? Anyfile previews its saved composite image in your browser, with zoom and pan. No Photoshop installation or account is needed, and your local file is never uploaded.

This is a composite-image preview: layers cannot be toggled or edited, and a saved composite is required. The limits are 256 MiB per file and 64 Mi pixels per composite. Some color modes and features may be unsupported.

[Open the PSB viewer](https://www.anyfile.top/en/formats/psb) · [Try the color-study sample](https://www.anyfile.top/en/view?entry=%2Fen%2Fformats%2Fpsb#file=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.anyfile.top%2Fsamples%2Fdesign%2Fcolor-study.psb)
