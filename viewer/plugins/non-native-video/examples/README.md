The declared scope is Chromium. Matroska, MPEG-TS and QuickTime use WebCodecs; Ogg Theora uses OGV.js software decoding. Matroska requires a Cues index. MPEG-TS is limited to one program containing one AVC/HEVC video stream and at most one AAC/MP3 audio stream; 188-byte TS and 192-byte M2TS packet layouts are covered. QuickTime is limited to AVC/HEVC plus PCM S16 or video-only. Ogg is limited to one Theora video plus Vorbis/Opus or video-only. HDR, exact color reproduction, subtitles, chapters and alternate-track selection are not claimed.
